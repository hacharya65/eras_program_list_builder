Quick Reference Guide #02

The Advising Review Pipeline & Relational Data

Replacing email ping-pong and scavenger hunts with deterministic software pipelines.

1. The 5-Stage Document Lifecycle (State Machine)

In a web application, an essay or experience is never an unmanaged file. It is a record with an explicit Status:

1. Drafting → 2. Submitted → 3. In Review → 4. Feedback Ready → 5. Approved
Stage What Student Sees What Advisor Sees System Action
Drafting Editable fields with real-time character caps. Nothing (student's private work-in-progress). Autosaves drafts safely in the database.
Submitted Fields lock to read-only; status badge says "Waiting in Queue". Appears in the advisor's incoming review inbox with timestamp. Sends alert to advisor; locks document editing.
In Review Status badge turns purple: "Currently being reviewed by Dr. X". Active split-screen: student draft on left, rubric/notes on right. Prevents two advisors from reviewing simultaneously.
Feedback Ready Notification received; student sees inline rubric & actionable notes. Archived in completed reviews history. Unlocks document if revisions are requested.
Approved Green checkmark: "Ready for 1-Click ERAS Export". Signed-off badge on Dean's cohort dashboard. Generates clean, pre-validated text formatted for ERAS.

2. Solving the Alumni "Scavenger Hunt" with Relational Data

In a spreadsheet or email thread, alumni data is disconnected. In a relational database, tables connect to each other through relationships:

The Relational Chain

ACGME Programs Table: Master list of 5,500+ verified residency programs (specialty, hospital, city, state).
↑ linked to
Alumni Match Table: Which graduates from your medical school matched there (Match Year, Specialty, Fellowship track).
↑ linked to
Alumni Contact Table: Willingness to be contacted, verified institutional email, LinkedIn, and peer advising tips.

Result: When a student adds "Massachusetts General Hospital - Internal Medicine" to their school list, the app instantly displays: "3 Alumni from your school matched here (2023, 2024, 2025). [View Contacts]"
