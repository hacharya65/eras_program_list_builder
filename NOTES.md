# Teaching Scratchpad & User Preferences

## User Profile & Preferences
- **Background**: Non-technical background.
- **Role**: Domain expert / Product lead in medical education & residency advising.
- **Teaching Style Preference**: High-clarity explanations, domain-grounded analogies (clinical/hospital/residency metaphors), visual representations, immediate hands-on wins.
- **Key Lived Pain Points**:
  1. **Document & Essay Sprawl**: Preparing dozens of essays/documents across divergent Google Docs, creating multiple version conflicts (`v1`, `v2_final`, `v3_edits`).
  2. **Email Ping-Pong & Communication Black Hole**: Students email documents to busy advisors and have zero visibility into whether it was received, where it is in the review queue, or when feedback will arrive.
  3. **The Pre-Season Gap**: ERAS doesn't open until June/July, forcing students to draft in Google Docs first and then manually copy-paste into ERAS (risking formatting loss and character cutoff errors).
  4. **The School List & Alumni Scavenger Hunt**: Finding historical match data for their specific medical school, tracking down alumni who matched at target programs, and securing accurate contact information is currently fragmented and painful.
- **Existing Asset**: An existing repository exists at `/Users/hacharya/Desktop/internal_eras_advising_portal` with Next.js 14, Supabase, and Gemini integration that can serve as a live lab.
