# ERAS Advising Web Application Resources

## Knowledge

- [Book: _Fundamentals of Software Architecture_ by Mark Richards & Neal Ford (O'Reilly)](file:///Users/hacharya/Desktop/AI%20Coding/OReilly.Fundamentals.of.Software.Architecture.2020.1.pdf)
  Foundational guide on software components, architectural characteristics, and client-server partitioning. Use for: mental models of how user interfaces, business logic, and databases divide responsibilities.
- [AAMC ERAS Applicant Worksheet & Official Guidelines](https://students-residents.aamc.org/applying-residencies-eras/applying-residencies-eras)
  The primary source specification for character counts (750 chars for descriptions, 300 chars for meaningful reflections), 10-experience caps, and 5 scholarly publication formats. Use for: validation rules and data schema definitions.
- [NRMP Charting Outcomes in the Match](https://www.nrmp.org/match-data-analytics/residency-data-reports/)
  The definitive biennial benchmark dataset detailing median Step 2 CK scores, research item counts, and contiguous rank lengths for matched vs unmatched seniors. Use for: competitiveness algorithms and radar benchmarks.
- [Supabase Architecture Overview](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/database/overview)
  High-trust, readable documentation explaining how modern relational databases (PostgreSQL) store user data, enforce Row-Level Security, and expose instantaneous APIs. Use for: understanding how student and advisor data is isolated and queried safely.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/medicalschool & r/Residency](https://reddit.com/r/medicalschool)
  Large community of active medical students navigating the ERAS application cycle in real time. Use for: discovering current pain points, signaling anxieties, and feedback on advising workflows.
- [AAMC Group on Student Affairs (GSA)](https://www.aamc.org/professional-development/affinity-groups/gsa)
  The professional community of medical school student affairs deans and residency advisors. Use for: understanding institutional oversight, compliance, and multi-student advising bottlenecks.
