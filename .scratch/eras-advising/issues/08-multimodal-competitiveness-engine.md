# 08: Multimodal Competitiveness Engine (Summary Statistics Integration)

**What to build:** An objective, data-driven competitiveness assessment engine that parses and utilizes concise population-level medians directly from `Summary Statistics.pdf` for all specialties. Students enter their credentials (Step 2 CK, clerkship honors, research output, AOA status) and see their profile compared against matched vs. unmatched national medians across all dimensions (Step 2 score, abstracts, presentations, publications, work/volunteer experiences, AOA rates). Includes direct hyperlinks to Residency Explorer on each program card for interview yield comparisons based on signaling.

**Blocked by:** 07: Strategic School List Matrix with Dynamic Specialty Signaling

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Population-level benchmark data ingested from `Summary Statistics.pdf` covering all ACGME specialties (matched vs unmatched medians).
- [ ] Student metric profile entry (USMLE Step 2 CK, research experiences, abstracts, presentations, publications, volunteer experiences, AOA membership).
- [ ] Multimodal benchmark indicator translating comparison data into intuitive ranges: *Comfortable*, *Competitive*, and *Stretch*.
- [ ] Deep links to Residency Explorer on program cards allowing students to check interview yield rates for Gold vs Silver vs No Signal.
